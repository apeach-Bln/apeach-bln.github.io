PokemonTCGTrackerBot

This page describes an automated program that reads public product pages of online shops. It is operated by a private individual in Germany.

What it does

It reads publicly available product information (title, price, stock status, product image) from online shops that sell collectible trading cards, mainly through the public products.json feed and collection pages of Shopify storefronts. The data is used for a private price and availability overview. It is not resold and not published.

How it behaves

  • It identifies itself with the User-Agent PokemonTCGTrackerBot/1.0 (+https://apeach-bln.github.io/).
  • Requests to Shopify storefronts are signed with Web Bot Auth (HTTP Message Signatures, Ed25519). The public key is at /.well-known/http-message-signatures-directory.
  • Each shop is read once every 3 to 24 hours, one request at a time, with a pause of at least 3 seconds between requests.
  • It follows each shop's robots.txt.
  • On HTTP 429 or 403 it stops for that shop and waits at least for the Retry-After period before trying again.
  • It does not log in, does not use accounts or carts, and does not try to get around bot protection.

Opt out / contact

If you operate a shop and do not want to be read by this bot, please open an issue at github.com/apeach-Bln/apeach-bln.github.io/issues with your shop's domain. It will be excluded.
